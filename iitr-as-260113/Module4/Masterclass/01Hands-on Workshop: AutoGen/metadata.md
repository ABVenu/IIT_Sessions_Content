title: Hands-on Workshop: AutoGen — Hotel Guest Complaint Intake Desk

objective: Build a Hotel Guest Complaint Intake Desk with AutoGen where specialised agents intake a guest complaint, classify it, look up the stay with a tool, create a ticket, and close the group chat under round and termination controls.

type of session: mixture of theory + implementation

topics be covered: AutoGen conversable agents; tools via register_function; GroupChat and GroupChatManager; speaker selection; max rounds; termination; hotel complaint intake hands-on


detailed subtopics to be covered:
* Design a hotel guest complaint intake workflow as a multi-agent team (intake → classify → desk clerk with tools) with clear role boundaries.
* Configure AssistantAgent specialists plus a UserProxyAgent executor; register lookup_guest_stay and create_complaint_ticket tools safely.
* Run a GroupChat with GroupChatManager, speaker selection, and max rounds so the team produces a ticket confirmation without runaway dialogue.
* Review the conversation trace for tool use, handoffs, and termination; diagnose one failure mode (wrong speaker or repetition) and apply a configuration fix.
* Complete an end-to-end walkthrough and a minimal AutoGen reliability checklist for the hotel intake desk.
