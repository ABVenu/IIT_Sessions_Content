lecture ID: TeachersDay2026

Course Name: AI Masterclass for School Students

Target Audience: Students from Classes 9 to 12; no laptop or coding background required

session duration: 2hr 15mins

Session Notes Length: detailed student lecture notes for an awareness and demo masterclass

title: AI in Daily Life

objective: Help students recognise AI already on their phones, see the E-Eye elephant video, use school tools (Gemini, Lens, NotebookLM, Stitch, a small published calendar), and meet strong field examples such as scan helpers and vehicles that notice animals.

type of session: theory with live demonstrations

topics be covered:
• What AI is, in plain language
• AI already on a phone: finger unlock, face unlock, word prediction
• This is AI vs this is not AI
• E-Eye elephant video: learned pictures vs a simple motion rule
• Daily tools: Gemini, Lens, voice typing, Translate, Maps, YouTube
• School-making tools: NotebookLM, Google Stitch, a Vercel-hosted calendar or to-do
• How to ask a clear question
• Strong field uses: breast-scan help, vehicles that notice animals, buses that slow down
• AI and careers
• Safe use

detailed subtopics to be covered:
• Finger unlock, face unlock, and next-word suggestion as everyday AI
• Not AI: calculator, simple paint app, alarm clock, torch
• Instructor demonstrations: unlock and next-word; elephant video; weak vs clear question; Lens; NotebookLM slides; Stitch poster; school calendar link; an answer that must be checked
• Individual notebook activities only: tick AI or not AI; rewrite one weak question
• Student uses: explain a chapter, extra practice, slides from own notes, project poster draft
• Fields: science (scan helper), road and rail safety, commerce, arts
• Career message: AI as a helper in every stream; teacher and driver and doctor still decide
• Safety: personal details, medical photos, exams, wrong answers, fake videos
